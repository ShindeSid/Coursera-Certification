# Simple Interest Calculator

## About the Project

The Simple Interest Calculator is a basic application that calculates simple interest based on the principal amount, annual interest rate, and time period. It makes the calculation process easier and faster without requiring manual calculations.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

Where:

* Principal is the initial amount of money.
* Rate is the annual interest rate in percentage.
* Time is the duration in years.

## Features

* Calculates simple interest based on user input.
* Displays the total amount, including interest.
* Provides a simple and easy-to-use interface.
* Reduces the need for manual calculations.

## How to Use

1. Enter the principal amount.
2. Enter the annual interest rate.
3. Enter the time period in years.
4. Click the Calculate button to calculate the simple interest.
5. View the simple interest and total amount.
